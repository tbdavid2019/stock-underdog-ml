# Forecast performance and model health

The read-only evaluation helpers consume settled forecast records and return deterministic summaries. `rank_stocks(records, PerformanceQuery(...))` reports one row per stock/model setup, while `rank_models(records, query)` compares model revisions on the shared ticker/session intersection. Configure `as_of` as an ISO-8601 timestamp with a timezone. The supported windows are 20, 60, and 120 market sessions; the default minimum sample count is 30.

Model comparison cohorts use market, horizon, target type, evidence type, and feature profile. A model's `config_hash` remains visible in its result but does not split otherwise comparable model samples. The model comparison selects the latest eligible forecast for each ticker and target session. It reports common-sample metrics and full-sample counts separately.

Price MAE, MAPE, and improvement against the current-price baseline remain price metrics. `oos_r2_zero_return` uses squared return error against a zero-return forecast, so its value does not depend on a stock's nominal price level. Direction accuracy uses the configured flat-return threshold. Wilson intervals in model summaries describe session-level outcomes and do not establish independent-stock statistical significance.

`evaluate_model_health(records, as_of=..., eligible_tickers_by_session=...)` aggregates each ticker's latest settled forecast within a target session, then gives each ticker and session equal weight. The supplied ticker map is the versioned eligible-universe denominator used for coverage. A session below the 80% default coverage threshold remains visible and does not count toward a trigger streak. Records whose actuals were unavailable at `as_of` are excluded.

Health policy begins in `observe_only`: `recommended_weight` reports the policy result while `effective_weight` preserves `current_weight`. `policy_mode="enforce"` applies the recommendation. The initial quality rules require at least 30 observations across 20 countable sessions, flag watch when recent performance triggers apply, degrade after three consecutive trigger sessions, and suspend after five. Suspension also follows when a deterministic 2,000-resample, five-session circular block bootstrap puts the hit-rate 95% upper bound below 0.5. Supply prior transition events through `prior_events` to enable recovery checks. Recovery samples must follow the suspension event; five qualifying sessions first restore degraded weight, and 20 further new qualifying sessions restore healthy weight. Transition events include an idempotency key, policy version, effective time, weights, and evidence summary. Re-evaluating the same snapshot after a transition does not count its earlier sessions again. The result includes per-session evidence and the coverage decision so callers can audit the recommendation. Service availability and market-data freshness are operational inputs and should be reported separately from this quality summary.

These helpers expect the caller to supply already settled records, a fixed evaluation cutoff, and an eligible-ticker snapshot. They do not fetch market data, persist health events, or rewrite previously applied weights.
